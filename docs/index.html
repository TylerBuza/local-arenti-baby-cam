A SMALL SETUP. A SHORTER PATH.

From the camera to your couch.

Full setup guide ↗
  1. 01

    Enable local access

    Set up the camera’s Wi-Fi, then enable password-protected ONVIF/RTSP if your firmware supports it.

  2. 02

    Find your camera

    Discover it in Windows and enter its local credentials. Test the video, then save your setup.

  3. 03

    Pair your phone

    On the same LAN, scan the QR in Phone access. Your phone gets its own certificate and reconnects automatically.

  4. 04

    Close the cloud path

    Once local viewing works, block the camera’s internet access at your router. Verify the result.

For reliable reconnects, reserve the camera and Windows PC’s LAN addresses in your router.

PRIVACY, BY DESIGN AND BY SETUP

A private room.
A smaller footprint.

Every cloud account and remote service adds another place where access can go wrong. Local viewing keeps the everyday video path on your network.

The other half is up to your router: the app doesn’t stop camera firmware from contacting the internet.

Read the isolation guide
01

Keep the useful connection.

Preserve camera-to-PC LAN access. Phone-to-PC viewing uses TLS and individual client certificates.

02

Block the unnecessary one.

Deny camera-to-WAN traffic across IPv4 and IPv6. Review port forwards, UPnP, DNS proxying and existing sessions.

03

Verify, then rely on it.

Check firewall logs and counters. Confirm local viewing still works. A vendor “offline” badge alone isn’t proof.

Camera-side RTSP/ONVIF is not encrypted. Use a trusted or segmented LAN, and plan for firmware maintenance.

Ask a question ↗
Will it work with my camera?

This beta targets Arenti/D3 cameras with password-protected ONVIF and RTSP already enabled. It cannot unlock a local interface your firmware doesn’t offer or derive a local password from your cloud login.

The core viewer has run on one D3. Generic setup is tested with synthetic fixtures; independent users’ hardware remains unverified. This is not a promise of support for every Arenti model.

Does my PC need to stay on?

Yes. Android connects through the Windows connector, so that PC must stay running and awake. After initial setup and pairing, both apps reconnect automatically using their saved configuration.

Can I watch away from home?

LAN-only viewing is the default. Optional VPN or Tailscale/Funnel configuration is separate and adds external services and another access boundary. Never expose the camera’s own ONVIF/RTSP ports to the internet.

What gets recorded or collected?

Windows saves a screenshot or video only when you ask. Android and the connector stream in memory. The apps contain no advertising or analytics, and public downloads contain no pre-enrolled camera or family access.

This website has no project analytics, remote fonts or third-party scripts. GitHub hosts it and may process request metadata under its own privacy policy.

Is this an official Arenti product?

No. This is an independent, open-source community project, not affiliated with Arenti. It provides no medical alerts or guaranteed monitoring uptime. Project code is MIT licensed; dependencies retain their own licenses.
